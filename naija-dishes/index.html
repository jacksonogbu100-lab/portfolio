About us

Premium natural taste

Naija Dishes cooks Nigerian meals for breakfast, lunch, and dinner at two Abuja spots: H-Medix Plaza on 2nd Avenue, Gwarinpa, and Shafa Fueling Station in Jahi.

The board is soups and swallow, jollof and fried rice, chicken stew, beans with egg, fruit parfait, and tea in the VIP section. Delivery runs anywhere in the city.
